\section{Related Work}
Quantization for inference has been explored extensively in SmoothQuant, GPTQ,
and AWQ, among others, focusing on accuracy-preserving weight-only and
activation-aware techniques. Systems efforts such as FlashInfer and TensorRT-LLM
emphasize kernel specialization and end-to-end inference throughput. CUTLASS and
cuBLASLt provide highly optimized GEMM kernels that serve as deployment
baselines. Tiny-GEMM complements these lines of work by focusing on the decode
regime and providing a measurement-based model of when weight-only INT4 improves
latency.
