\subsection{A Regime Model for INT4 Decode GEMMs}
We model total latency as:
\begin{equation}
T_{\text{total}} = T_{\text{launch}} + T_{\text{mem}}(W) + T_{\text{dequant}} + T_{\text{compute}}.
\end{equation}
Narrow projections are dominated by fixed $T_{\text{dequant}}$ and limited
parallelism, while wide FFN layers are dominated by $T_{\text{mem}}$. INT4
reduces $T_{\text{mem}}$, creating a transition boundary in arithmetic intensity
space. Figure~\ref{fig:regime-boundary} visualizes this boundary.

\begin{figure}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{regime_boundary.png}
  \caption{Regime boundary: speedup vs arithmetic intensity.}
  \label{fig:regime-boundary}
\end{figure}
